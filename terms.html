Terms

The deal, without the legal fog.

Kernel is a volunteer project. Every command is free. Premium and VIP are optional support tiers, not a licence for extra protection. By adding the bot or using this site you accept the points below.

Read the privacy page

1 · Free tier

Anti-nuke, automod, logging, moderation cases, verification, economy and games are available on every server at no cost. There is no trial, no locked command and no member limit promised. If a feature is listed on the site, it is in the bot for everyone.

2 · Premium and VIP

These tiers buy a Discord role, priority answers and a say in the roadmap. They do not buy protection, extra data, uptime guarantees or admin access to anyone else's server. Payment happens on an external payment page run by a third-party provider; the role is granted by hand after a receipt.

Subscriptions are monthly and do not renew automatically from this site. If the project stops, the role stops with it. If something is wrong, ask for a refund within the month you paid — send a message.

3 · Acceptable use

  • Do not use Kernel to harass, mass-DM, raid or evade a Discord ban.
  • Do not attempt to extract tokens, settings or data belonging to another server.
  • Do not resell access or present the bot as your own product.
  • Follow the Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines — they override anything here.

Servers that break these rules can be blocked from using the bot. Blocking a server is not a refund event for a voluntary support tier.

4 · Availability

Kernel runs on a single host and is maintained in spare time. Commands can be offline during a restart, a host outage or a Discord API incident. The status page shows the last probe results rather than a promise. Nothing here is an SLA.

5 · Your data

What is stored is listed on the privacy page. You can ask for a review, a case or a Premium grant tied to your account to be deleted, and the owner will delete it. Deleting the bot from a server removes that server's settings on the next cleanup.

6 · Liability and affiliation

Kernel is provided as is, without warranty. The owner is not liable for moderation decisions made by your staff, for Discord outages, or for damage caused by a compromised staff account. Kernel is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discord Inc.
