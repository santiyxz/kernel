Payments happen on a separate payment page, never on this site — Kernel never sees a card number. After paying, open a ticket in the support server with your Discord username so the role is granted.

01 · Pick a tier

Premium or VIP. Both are per month and cancel by simply not renewing; nothing is taken automatically by this site.

02 · Pay on the payment page

The button opens the owner's payment page (Ko-fi, PayPal or similar). The card details stay with that provider.

03 · Get the role

Open a ticket in the support server with your Discord username and a receipt. The role is granted by hand, usually the same day.

What you are not buying

  • Not protection. Anti-nuke, automod, logging and every command work exactly the same on the free tier.
  • Not data. Kernel stores no card number, no password and no login history. A grant records your Discord user id and tier so the role can be checked.
  • Not a contract. If the project stops, the role stops with it. Ask for a refund within the month if something is wrong — contact the owner.

Questions before paying? Send a message or open a ticket in the support server.
