    Before you write

    • Is the bot down or slow? Check the public status page first — it shows uptime, gateway ping and the aggregate server count.
    • Is a command misbehaving? Include the command name, what you expected and what happened.
    • Never paste a bot token, an ngrok authtoken or a panel password. Nobody here will ask for one.
