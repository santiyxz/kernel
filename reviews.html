How reviews are handled

  • Sign-in required. The bot checks your Discord token server-side; there is no anonymous posting and no email form.
  • No editing by the owner. A review can be hidden only for tokens, personal data, spam or harassment, and the reason is recorded.
  • One per account. Submitting again updates your previous review instead of stacking a second one.

Something wrong with a review? Send a message with the username and what to look at.
